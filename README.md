# Privacy Policy for GST Invoice Maker

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bilsoft** ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information in the **GST Invoice Maker** mobile application (the "App"). 

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is designed to be simple and transparent, especially for our business users in India.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**A. Business and Customer Data (Stored Locally):**
The App is designed to work offline. All data you enter into the App—including your business details, customer names, phone numbers, addresses, GSTIN, product lists, invoices, and payment records—is stored **locally on your device** in a secure database. We do not upload, view, or store your business or customer data on any external servers.

**B. Login Information:**
To secure your account, we use third-party authentication services (Google Firebase). When you log in using Google or Email, Firebase may collect your email address and basic profile information. We do not have access to your passwords.

**C. Backup Data:**
The App allows you to create backup files of your data. These files are generated locally on your device. If you choose to share these files (e.g., via WhatsApp, Google Drive, or Email), the security and privacy of that file become subject to the terms of the third-party service you use.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we have access to (mostly your email for login) solely for the following purposes:
- To authenticate you and allow you to access the App.
- To ensure the App functions correctly and securely.
- To provide customer support if you contact us at **bilsoft.mailus@gmail.com**.

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third parties.

---

### 3. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Because your business data is stored locally on your device, its safety depends on your device's security (e.g., screen lock, device encryption). We recommend keeping your device secure and regularly taking backups using the App's built-in backup feature.

---

### 4. Your Rights and Choices

- **Data Deletion:** Since your data is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can delete individual records or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
- **Logout:** You can securely log out of the App at any time using the menu.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for business use and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
